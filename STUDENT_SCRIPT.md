# Student instructions script (~90 seconds)

Read this while the dashboard is projected with results hidden. It follows `INSTRUCTOR_NOTES.md`: say that groups are random and there are no right answers, and nothing about strategies, favoritism, or what the two parts differ in.

## Script

> We're going to do a short activity on your phones. It takes about five minutes. It's anonymous: no names, no logins, and nothing that identifies you is collected.
>
> Scan the QR code on the screen, or type in the web address under it.
>
> When the page opens, it puts you in one of two groups, **Purple** or **Green**. The page assigns groups at random, like a coin flip.
>
> Then you'll split points between two people. Each person is shown only as a code number and a group. You don't get any of the points yourself.
>
> The screen shows a list of options. Each option is one way to split the points. The labels at the top tell you which number goes to which person. Read them, because the layout isn't the same on everyone's phone.
>
> Tap the option you want, then tap **Confirm**. You'll do this twice. Once you confirm, you can't change your answer.
>
> There are no right or wrong answers. Please work on your own and don't compare screens with your neighbors. We'll talk about it together once everyone is done.
>
> When you see **"Thanks. Watch the screen,"** you're finished. Put your phone down and look up here.

## Short version (for the slide or if you're short on time)

1. Scan the QR code.
2. You're randomly put in Purple or Green.
3. Split points between two people. You get none yourself.
4. Check the labels at the top. Tap an option, then tap **Confirm**. You'll do this twice.
5. No right answers. Work on your own.
6. When you see "Watch the screen," you're done.

## Likely questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Who are these people?" | "Other people in the activity. You only see a code and a group." (Don't add more.) |
| "Do I get points?" | "No, you don't get any." |
| "Which one should I pick?" | "Whatever you want. There's no right answer." |
| "Why am I Purple/Green?" | "It's random." |
| "The page says *Not sent yet*." | "Tap **Try again**. Your answers are saved on your phone." (Usually a wifi or cellular issue.) |
| "The page says *Submissions are closed*." | It came in after you closed submissions. Don't reopen for one person. |
| "I closed the tab / reloaded." | "Open the link again. It picks up where you left off." (Same browser on the same phone.) |
| "Can I do it again?" | "No, once per person." |

## Don't say

- Anything about "favoring," "your team," "competing," or "fairness." That tells students what you're measuring.
- What makes the two parts different (costly vs. free).
- What other groups or classes chose before you reveal the results.
